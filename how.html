How It Works

Discover how to connect easily with startups and researchers. Our platform simplifies the process of finding the right connections for your needs.

For Startups

Startup Community Connection Overview

    • Startups must register using Aadhar and PAN card verification.

  • Profile Creation:

  • Community Interaction:

For Researchers

Here are five key points on how a researcher can approach verification requirements and engage with the community:

1. Verification Process

  • Enrolment Number: Collect the enrolment number from participants for identity verification.
  • Institution Name: Ensure to include the name of the college or institution for authenticity.

2. Identification Documents

  • Aadhar Card: Request a copy of the Aadhar card to confirm identity and residency.
  • PAN Card: Include the PAN card for financial and identity verification purposes.

3. Community Engagement

  • Feedback Mechanism: Establish a platform (like forums or surveys) where community members can ask questions or provide suggestions.
  • Open Dialogue: Encourage a two-way dialogue for participants to express concerns or share insights related to the research topic.

4. Ethical Considerations

  • Informed Consent: Ensure participants understand how their information will be used and gain their consent.
    • Confidentiality: Assure participants that their data will be kept confidential and used solely for research purposes.

5. Writing and Communication

  • Clear Instructions: Provide clear guidelines on how to submit verification documents and participate in the research.
  • Professional Tone: Maintain a respectful and professional tone in all communication to foster trust and cooperation from the community.
